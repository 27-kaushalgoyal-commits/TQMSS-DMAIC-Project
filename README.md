# TQMSS-DMAIC-Project

## Six Sigma Quality Improvement: Reducing Manufacturing Defects in Wooden Toy Production

### Project Overview

This project applies the **Six Sigma DMAIC (Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control)** methodology to identify and reduce manufacturing defects in wooden toy production at Chandan Wooden Toys Pvt. Ltd.

The project uses a 28-day production and quality dataset to evaluate defects across three major categories:

- Carving / Shaping
- Paint / Finish
- Joint / Assembly

The project was completed as part of the **Total Quality Management & Six Sigma (TQMSS)** course.

---

## Business Problem

During the 28-day baseline period:

- **Total production:** 10,909 toys
- **Total defective:** 593 toys
- **Baseline defect rate:** 5.44%
- **Baseline yield:** 94.56%
- **DPMO:** 18,119.6
- **Project target:** Reduce defect rate toward **≤ 2.50%**

The objective is to reduce manufacturing defects while maintaining production volume.

---

## DMAIC Approach

### 1. Define

The project defined the manufacturing quality problem, project goal, CTQ, scope and stakeholders.

**CTQ:** Defect-free wooden toy with acceptable carving/shaping, paint/finish and joint/assembly quality.

**Scope includes:** Carving/shaping, paint/finish, joint/assembly, inspection, defect recording and rework/rejection.

**Scope excludes:** Marketing, pricing, distribution and customer-service activities outside manufacturing quality.

---

### 2. Measure

The 28-day dataset was analyzed to establish baseline performance.

| KPI | Baseline |
|---|---:|
| Total Production | 10,909 |
| Total Defective | 593 |
| Defect Rate | 5.44% |
| Yield | 94.56% |
| DPMO | 18,119.6 |

A P-chart was used to evaluate process stability. Three special-cause points were identified on **Days 8, 15 and 21**.

---

### 3. Analyze

Pareto analysis identified the major defect contributors:

| Rank | Defect Category | Defects | Share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | Paint / Finish | 277 | 46.7% |
| 2 | Joint / Assembly | 178 | 30.0% |
| 3 | Carving / Shaping | 138 | 23.3% |

Paint/Finish was identified as the first improvement priority. Paint/Finish and Joint/Assembly together represented **76.7% of recorded defects**.

Fishbone analysis and 5 Why analysis were used to investigate possible causes. The leading root-cause hypothesis was **insufficient standard work and visual controls**, particularly around paint application, drying and acceptance criteria.

---

### 4. Improve

The project proposed the following improvement actions:

1. Standard Paint/Finish SOP and visual defect standard
2. Minimum drying time and first-piece approval
3. Assembly fixture / poka-yoke
4. Daily tool check and replacement trigger
5. Common defect-definition guide

FMEA was used to prioritize risks.

The highest RPNs were:

- **Incorrect paint application — RPN 280 — High priority**
- **Insufficient drying — RPN 252 — High priority**
- **Wrong joint positioning — RPN 200 — Medium priority**

---

### 5. Control

The proposed control plan focuses on sustaining improvements through:

- Daily defect-rate and category monitoring
- P-chart and KPI monitoring
- First-piece approval for every batch
- Weekly SOP compliance audits
- Green / Yellow / Red reaction logic
- Corrective action when abnormal conditions are identified

The project target is **≤2.50% defect rate**.

> Note: The project does not claim that the 2.50% target has been achieved. Post-improvement pilot data is required to confirm the result.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how a structured Six Sigma approach can be used to understand a manufacturing quality problem, identify improvement priorities, investigate potential root causes and establish controls for sustaining future improvements.

The next step is to run the proposed pilot and compare post-improvement defect performance against the **5.44% baseline** before concluding whether the **≤2.50% target** has been achieved.
